🎡 Whiff-End Pier — music audition

Four candidate tracks to replace v1's "Carnival Rides" (Damien's verdict: awful). All four are CC0 / public domain — licence checked directly on the OpenGameArt.org hosting page for each, not just the metadata tags. Play each one and pick your favourite; whichever one you choose gets renamed to pier.mp3 and the other three (plus this page) can be deleted.

Note: the current audio/music/pier.mp3 ("Carnival Rides") has not been touched or replaced — it's still the live track until you choose one of these.

1Insert Coin

Author: megupets

Source: https://opengameart.org/content/insert-coin

Licence evidence: page's "License(s):" field shows CC0 only (no CC-BY/SA alternative listed). Also filed in the "8-bit Chiptune commercial ok", "CC0 Chiptunes" and "CC0 Music" collections.

Picked on (I cannot hear it — this is text evidence only): tagged 8-bit / chiptune / "title screen". Author's own description: a "simple title screen song with chiptune/8bit style". Straightforward, punchy NES-style arcade-cabinet energy — the most literally "arcade" of the four.

Source loop was 51s; looped ×2 with a 1s crossfade to reach 1:41, well inside the 1–3 min spec.

2Bonus Round — 8bit

Author: Wolfgang_

Source: https://opengameart.org/content/bonus-round-8bit

Licence evidence: page's "License(s):" field shows CC0 only. Filed in the "8-bit Game Music" and "CC0 - Retro Music" collections.

Picked on (text evidence only): tagged 8bit / retro / "minigames". Author's own description: a "bonus round/minigame theme made with Famitracker" — a different chiptune engine/composer to Candidate 1, so a genuinely different flavour of 8-bit, not a reskin. Should read as bouncier/gamier — think a mini-game sting stretched into a loop.

3We Are Prophet — Happy, Energetic Tune

Author: TinyWorlds

Source: https://opengameart.org/content/we-are-prophet-happy-energetic-tune

Licence evidence: page's "License(s):" field shows CC0 only. Filed in "Audio - CC0 - 8Bit -Chiptune", "CC0 Chiptunes" and "CC0 Music" collections.

Picked on (text evidence only): the track's own title literally contains "Happy, Energetic Tune", and its tags are happy, funny, fun, energy, energetic, 8-bit, chiptune — the most directly on-brief title of the four for "silly upbeat romp". Made with Autotracker for a game jam, so expect something rougher/scrappier than the others — that's the variety pick.

Source loop was 35s (the shortest of the four); looped ×3 with 1s crossfades to reach 1:42.

4Bouncy Hamster Dancing (Menu Music?)

Author: cynicmusic

Source: https://opengameart.org/content/bouncy-hamster-dancing-menu-music

Licence evidence: page's "License(s):" field shows CC0 only. Filed in "CC0 Upbeat / Electronic Music" and "Music - Platform & Background" collections.

Picked on (text evidence only): tagged catchy / infectious / menu. Author's own words: a "ridiculously infectious theme" deliberately reminiscent of the old Hamster Dance meme — the daftest premise of the four by name alone. Made with a different toolset to the other three (synth/electronic rather than pure NES-chiptune), so this is the instrumentation outlier — a good contrast if the other three feel too samey.

Source loop was 65s; looped ×2 with a 1s crossfade to reach 2:09.
